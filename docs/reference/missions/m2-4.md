**Key insight:** Bounds certified. MIN and MAX are the same aggregation family as SUM, COUNT, and AVG — they just answer "smallest" and "largest" instead of "total" or "how many," and now any invoice outside $0.99–$25.86 is an instant red flag instead of a guess.
